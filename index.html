Healthcare Expertise

Experience where complexity matters.

Healthcare is a core area of expertise, not the limit of what we do. Our experience includes the operational, regulatory, technical, and human complexities that shape successful healthcare environments.

Representative areas of experience include hospital expansion, imaging and diagnostic services, ground-up facilities, specialized clinical environments, programming and feasibility, and code and life safety review.

Sketch studies are conceptual illustrations of the service lines and expertise described here.

HOSPITAL EXPANSION

Supporting additions and vertical expansions that maintain operations and integrate new spaces.

IMAGING & DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES

Planning for imaging, interventional, and diagnostic environments with a focus on workflow and technology.

GROUND-UP FACILITIES

Supporting new facilities from early planning and programming through design and delivery.

SPECIALIZED CLINICAL ENVIRONMENTS

Operating rooms, labor and delivery, ICUs, pharmacies, and other high-acuity spaces.

PROGRAMMING & FEASIBILITY

Assessing needs, defining programs, evaluating opportunities, and developing the case for smart decisions.

CODE & LIFE SAFETY

Reviews and analysis for IBC, LSC, FGI, CMS, and Georgia DCH requirements.

Experience & Credentials
